Each file is a subset of a font from the google/fonts repository, renamed to 'Konto Sign <id>' because it was
modified (subset). Faces marked OFL-1.1 are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org);
faces marked Apache-2.0 are under the Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
The fonts may be bundled with the game; they may not be sold on their own.

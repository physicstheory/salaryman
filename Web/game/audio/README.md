# Karaoke songs

Drop the Salary Man theme here as **`salary-man.mp3`** (`.m4a`, `.ogg` or `.wav` also work). It is the company song at
company karaoke and in every karaoke box (`Web/game/karaoke.js`).

- Get it from Suno: open https://suno.com/s/zTbfyxll05N98gxu, the three-dot menu, Download, MP3 Audio.
- Optional lyrics: `salary-man.lrc`, one line per lyric with its start time, e.g. `[00:12.40] first line of verse one`.
  They scroll under the note ribbons.
- No file here? The game also finds a song called "Salary Man" in `Add Your Files\music`, then tries to stream it from Suno
  when online, and otherwise plays a built-in guide arrangement, so karaoke always works.

The ribbons (pitch and timing) are worked out from the audio when the song loads, so any song dropped in works.

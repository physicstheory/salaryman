# Salary Man: Viggle prompts for the Mission 1 cutscenes

Viggle animates a character image with a motion. In **Mix** you give it a character image and a short reference video of the movement (you can film yourself on a phone). In **Move** or **Animate** you give it the character and a text prompt. Every scene below has three parts:

1. **Character image.** Take a clean in-game screenshot of the character. Use F2 / photo mode on Ultra, a plain background (a wall or the sky), and the whole body in frame, standing straight. Or have an image tool make a full-body turnaround from the "Character sheet" text below.
2. **Motion.** What to film yourself doing for Mix, or what to type for Move.
3. **Scene prompt.** Paste this for the background and the camera. Keep each clip at 5 to 8 seconds, which is the length Viggle handles well. Cut the clips together after.

The look throughout: **modern anime, TV-key-visual quality, saturated, golden light, soft bloom, crisp ink lines, cel-shaded with two tones, painted backgrounds**. Think "slice-of-life anime opening sequence", not chibi and not photoreal.

## Scene 2: "Today's the day" (Seiko leaves the house)
- **Character:** Seiko, outfit A, bag on his shoulder.
- **Motion (Mix):** step forward out of a doorway, adjust the bag strap, take a breath and look up at the sky, then walk toward the camera with a small smile.
- **Scene prompt:**
```
Anime style. Morning in a Japanese farming village, a wooden house with a sliding door and a potted plant, warm golden backlight, dust motes glowing, a low tracking camera in front of him moving backward as he walks. Saturated colours, soft bloom, crisp line art.
```

## Scene 3: The farewell at the halt
- **Characters:** Mother and sister waving, Father with folded arms. Make three clips on the same background and composite them, or use Viggle's multi-character mode.
- **Motion:** Mother waves with both hands and calls out. Sister jumps and waves wildly. Father stays still, then gives one slow nod.
- **Scene prompt:**
```
Anime style. A tiny rural railway platform at sunrise: a wooden bench, a station name board, a vending machine glowing, cherry petals drifting across, mountains behind, the sun low and warm behind the family, so they're rim-lit in gold. A gentle handheld camera, shallow depth of field.
```

- **Characters:** Akio behind a long black desk; Seiko bowing deeply.

```
Anime style. A minimalist top-floor CEO office with floor-to-ceiling windows over a city skyline in morning light, a long black desk, a red digital clock reading 09:00:01 on the wall. Dramatic shadows, the tension of a quiet room, a slow dolly toward the desk.
```

---

**Tips**
- Use the **same character image in every clip** of a scene, or the face drifts.
- If Viggle adds a 3D look, add "flat cel-shaded anime, no 3D render, no realism" to the scene prompt.
- Plain backgrounds in the character image give the cleanest cut-outs. The in-game Ultra screenshots of Seiko against the sky work well.
- Export at 1080p and upscale to 4K afterwards (Topaz or CapCut) if Viggle caps the resolution.

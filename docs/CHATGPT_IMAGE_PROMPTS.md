# Salary Man — image prompts for ChatGPT image generation

Paste each block below into ChatGPT (image generation) as its own message. Each is written to stand alone, so there is nothing to attach. The island map is the only one that benefits from a follow-up.

Common ground for all three: **Salary Man** is an open-world game set on **Konto**, a fictionalised Honshu: a long, bent, mountainous island with the capital **Tōkonto** on a bay in the south-east. You play **Seiko Shigu**, a salaryman. The tone is satirical-but-affectionate Japan: tidy, crowded, a little absurd.

---

## 1. The island map (game-themed, ends at the ocean)

```
Create a top-down illustrated map of a fictional island called KONTO, a fictionalised version of Honshu, Japan. Style: a polished video-game world map, like the pause-menu map of a modern open-world game, but with a warm, slightly retro-travel-poster feel. Flat colour with soft shading, no photographic textures, no 3D perspective. Landscape orientation, 3:2.

THE OCEAN IS THE EDGE OF THE MAP. The island sits in the middle of the frame and open ocean surrounds it on every side, all the way to the borders of the image. Nothing extends past the sea: no land at the frame edge, no roads running off the paper. Deep blue at the edges fading to turquoise shallows around the coast, with subtle wave lines and a thin pale beach ring. Add a few small offshore islets and one or two short bridge links to near islets, but NO long bridges or causeways running out into open ocean.

THE ISLAND: long, narrow and slightly bowed, running south-west to north-east, with a mountainous spine down the middle (green foothills rising to grey-brown ridges with a few snow-capped peaks). Rivers run from the spine to the coast. Paddy-field patchwork on the lowlands, dark green forest on the slopes. One large bay on the south-east coast holds the capital TŌKONTO as a dense grey-and-yellow city block cluster with a few tall towers.

TOWNS AND LABELS (clean white sans-serif with a thin dark outline, small and tidy): TŌKONTO (capital, largest, south-east bay), MIZUHO (small village in the south-west lowlands, with a tiny house icon: this is the player's home), and four or five other towns placed along the coast and valleys. A compass rose in the lower-left ocean corner and a small scale bar. A title cartouche in the upper-left ocean corner reading "KONTO" with the subtitle "Salary Man".

ROADS: thin pale lines connecting towns, with ONE thicker golden-yellow highway running the length of the island between Mizuho and Tōkonto. No white or yellow centre-lines on ordinary roads; ordinary roads are plain cream strokes. Rail lines as thin dark dashed lines with small station dots.

Do not include real-world place names, real logos, or any text other than the labels above. No people, no vehicles.
```

**Follow-up if the coastline looks wrong:** *"Keep the same style, but make sure the ocean fills the entire border of the picture and no land touches the frame. Make the island narrower and more bowed."*

## 2. The Nihon Rail and bus network map (subway-style diagram)

```
Create a transit network diagram in the style of a Tokyo Metro / JR East system map: a clean schematic, not geographic. White background, landscape 3:2. Lines drawn with thick rounded strokes that run only horizontally, vertically or at 45 degrees. Stations are white circles with a dark outline; interchange stations are larger white circles with a thick black outline. Station names in a clean bold sans-serif with the Japanese name above the English name in smaller type.

THE SYSTEM is called NIHON RAIL, for the fictional island of Konto. Draw SIX train lines in distinct colours: a green trunk line, an orange loop line around the capital, a blue coastal line, a red express line, a purple mountain line, and a yellow airport line. Each line has a coloured circle badge with a letter-and-number code (G1, O1, B1, R1, P1, Y1) at both ends. Show at least four interchange stations where lines meet.

THE BUS SYSTEM is overlaid as thinner grey dashed lines with small square stops, feeding the stations from towns that have no train. Label bus routes with small rounded-rectangle badges ("Bus 12", "Bus 40", and so on).

Stations to include (all fictional): TŌKONTO CENTRAL (the main interchange, drawn largest), TŌKONTO BAY, SHINKUBA, KANDAGAWA, MIZUHO (end of the green line), and eight or nine more with plausible Japanese names. Add a legend box in the lower-right listing the six lines and the bus symbol, and a title strip across the top reading "NIHON RAIL  日本レール  Route Map".

Do not use any real railway company logos, real station names, or real line colours' branding. No people, no photographic elements.
```

## 3. Cover art

```
Create vertical (2:3) key art for a video game called SALARY MAN. Style: stylised, cinematic illustration in the spirit of a modern open-world crime game's box art, but warmer and funnier. Painterly with crisp graphic shapes, a sunset palette of orange, magenta and deep teal.

The hero is SEIKO SHIGU, a Japanese salaryman in his thirties: white dress shirt with the sleeves rolled, loose black tie, black trousers, tired but determined face, briefcase in one hand and a phone in the other. He stands front and centre, full figure, three-quarter view, confident posture.

Behind him, a sprawling night-time Tokyo-like skyline (the fictional capital, Tōkonto) with glowing glass towers, elevated expressways with streaks of car tail-lights, a bullet-train crossing a bridge, vending machines glowing on the street, and a few neon signs with Japanese characters. A huge setting sun low on the horizon. Small cartoonish touches: a police car chasing a delivery scooter in the mid-ground, a salaryman asleep on a bench, a cat on a station roof.

At the bottom, the logo "SALARY MAN" in bold white letters set on a rounded rectangular Japanese train-station signboard, with a thin coloured stripe along the top of the sign, and the smaller line "A game about commuting too far" beneath it. Leave the top 12% of the image as calm sky so a publisher strip can be added.

No real brands, no real logos, no real company names. Only the text "SALARY MAN" and the tagline.
```

**Variation for a wide banner (16:9):** *"Same artwork, recomposed horizontally: Seiko on the left third, the skyline sweeping right, logo on the sign bottom-right."*
